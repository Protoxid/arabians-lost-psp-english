# Arabians Lost: The Engagement on Desert — Localization Guide & Style Rules

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Character Authenticity:** Aileen Olazabal is a fierce, capable, sharp-tongued criminal princess. She must never sound like a generic, meek heroine.
2. **Distinct Voices:** Each suitor has a distinct rhythm, vocabulary, and social dynamic:
   - **Curtis:** Deceptively polite, smiling assassin; soft-spoken lethal charm.
   - **Roberto:** Energetic, brazen, streetwise gambler and charismatic tycoon.
   - **Shark:** Laconic, analytical, stern merchant; speaks in concise business terms.
   - **Stuart:** Aloof, aristocratic, condescending, cleanly articulated cynicism.
   - **Tyrone:** Brash, intense, visceral honesty; hot-headed and passionate.
   - **Lyle:** Dry, sarcastic, exasperated mentor; calls Aileen "Milady".
3. **Natural Localization:** Avoid literal Japanese syntax (omitted subjects must be reconstructed naturally without filler; Japanese particles and idioms localized faithfully).
4. **Tone & Setting:** Arabian fantasy meets criminal underworld. Maintain romantic tension, dark humor, threats, and intrigue without censorship or unwarranted embellishment.

---

## 2. Formatting & Technical Display Rules

1. **Dialogue Box Limitations:**
   - Width: ~38 to 42 characters per line before word wrapping.
   - Maximum lines per message window: 3 lines.
   - Total text per message: ~120 characters maximum.
2. **Line Breaks:**
   - Use `[br]` to break lines cleanly between sentences or natural grammatical pauses.
   - Never allow words to split awkwardly across box edges.
3. **Dialogue Quotes:**
   - Japanese spoken quotes `「 ... 」` must be mapped to English double quotes `" ... "`.
   - Inner thoughts / narration without Japanese brackets must NOT use quotation marks.
4. **Dynamic Placeholders:**
   - `[firstname]`: Dynamically replaced in-game by the player's chosen heroine name (default: "Aileen"). Never remove or alter this token.
5. **Character Encoding:**
   - All translated `.ks` files must encode cleanly to CP932 (Shift-JIS).
   - Use standard ASCII quotes `"`, apostrophes `'`, dashes `-`, and standard ASCII ellipses `...` (or three periods). Avoid Unicode non-CP932 characters.
